How to play

Three drums

The road ahead does not exist. Every rune you strike correctly lays the next stone under your feet.

  • Earth — a square rune. Lays a low stone.
  • Storm — a diamond. Lays a middle stone.
  • Sun — a circle. Lays a high stone.

Strike the matching drum as the rune crosses the glowing line. The first bar is a free warm-up.

Feel How to play
  • Each rune is a note of the song. Strike its drum as it crosses the line.
  • Hits keep the melody playing and lay the road. Misses mute it and crack the road.
  • A thorn beside a rune punishes the wrong drum.
  • Hits earn shards for the store. A ringed rune hides a relic.
