# Mythic Run

A rhythm runner for the browser. The road ahead doesn't exist until you drum it: strike the Earth, Storm and Sun drums on the beat and each correct hit lays the next stone under your feet.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder (for example with GitHub Pages). `hero.webp` holds the character's sprite frames and must sit next to `index.html`.

- **Touch:** tap the three drums.
- **Keyboard:** `A` `S` `D` for the drums, `Esc` or `P` to pause, `←` `→` to browse chapters on the home screen.

## What's in it

- **Story:** ten chapters across an AG apocalypse. Chapters 1 to 9 have ten levels each; chapter 10 is a single boss fight.
- **Endless:** pick any song you own and run until the road gives way.
- Seven original songs synthesised live with the Web Audio API, daily missions, eight collectible relics, a music shop and character skins.

Progress is saved in the browser's local storage. The shared leaderboard only works inside Claude's artifact viewer; elsewhere it shows your own best scores.
